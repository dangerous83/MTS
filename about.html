About MTS

International forwarders with Hamburg at our core.

The MTS story

More than two decades of moving cargo out of Hamburg.

Mangal Transport & Shipping GmbH — MTS — has been a Hamburg-based international freight forwarder since 2003. From our operational base at Billstrasse 158 in Rothenburgsort, a short drive from the container terminals of the Port of Hamburg, we plan, prepare and dispatch cargo to Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and every country of the Near and Middle East.

Our work is practical: customs clearance for goods entering or leaving the European Union, careful loading of vehicles and machinery into containers, cross-docking and consolidation of groupage, and door-to-door coordination by road, sea and air. Whether it is a single classic car, a groupage container to Dubai or a project shipment to West Africa, the same operational team owns the file from booking to arrival.

We work worldwide with a network of long-standing partner forwarders so that a shipment leaving our yard in Hamburg is met by people we know at the other end. That partnership model is why MTS files rarely surprise the customer at destination.

What we do

Three divisions, one operational file.

Each file at MTS runs through a single team that stays with the shipment from booking through arrival.

Customs & warehousing

Export declarations, T1/T2 procedures and bonded storage. We handle non-EU cargo under customs supervision, hold it in our warehouse and re-export when the paperwork is ready.

Vehicle logistics

Multi-car loading of 4–6 vehicles into a 40' HC container, careful lashing, photo documentation, RoRo bookings and single-vehicle premium shipments. We have our own container side loader and truck fleet for loadings in and around Hamburg and Bremerhaven.

International freight

FCL and LCL sea freight worldwide, road groupage into Europe, air freight and project cargo. Consolidation of LCL shipments directly from our Hamburg warehouse, in cooperation with partner forwarders.
