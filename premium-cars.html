Questions we hear often

Premium shipping FAQs.

Why a single-vehicle container?

So the loading plan is built around your car, not around what else fits. No shared bulkheads, no shared schedule, no risk of another car's mirror brushing yours during securing.

What about marine insurance?

We can quote a dedicated marine policy on request, based on the declared value. We'll walk you through excess, cover limits and documentation before you decide.

Can I visit the car in your warehouse before loading?

Yes. Our warehouse at Billstrasse 158 is a short drive from the Port of Hamburg. Call ahead and we'll arrange a time.

How do I know what's happening during the passage?

On the day the container sails you receive the vessel name, voyage number and ETA. We proactively flag any schedule changes.
